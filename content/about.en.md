This site is written by one person who runs the servers described in the guides: a handful of
VPS instances in Europe, a small Kubernetes cluster, a Nextcloud install, an internet radio and a
few voice gadgets on a desk.

Every command in a guide here was run on a machine that someone pays for, usually me. When
something broke, the guide says so. When I have not tested a step, the guide says that too.

What this site is not: a news aggregator, a reprint of vendor documentation, or a place for
sponsored posts dressed up as reviews. Some links are affiliate links, always marked as such:
see the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
