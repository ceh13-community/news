Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you sign up for a service through one of them, the
provider pays this site a commission. You pay the same price either way.

Rules I keep:

- A provider is only recommended if it runs something of mine in production, or the text says
  plainly that it was not tested.
- Affiliate links are marked with `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and routed through `/go/<provider>/`,
  so you can always see where a link goes before you click.
- Negative findings stay in the article. A commission never removes a drawback from a review.
- No paid placements, no guest posts, no link insertions.

Current partner programmes: Infomaniak, Proton, DigitalOcean, Vultr, Hetzner. The list changes when
the stack changes.
